 ### 🚨 Critical Security Vulnerabilities

  #### 1. Bypass of Razorpay Signature Verification in Production (Critical)

  • Location: subscription_payment.go:241 & tagatower.go:690
  • Code:
    isMock := config.Config.DisablePayment || strings.HasPrefix(req.OrderID, "mock_order_") ||
  req.Signature == "mock_signature"

  • The Bug:
  Because || (OR) is used without scoping to DisablePayment, even when running in live
  production (DisablePayment == false), if any client sends razorpay_signature:
  "mock_signature" or an order ID starting with mock_order_, isMock evaluates to true!
  • Impact:
  Anyone can forge a payment confirmation without paying a single rupee. The system will
  bypass signature verification, mark their annual subscription as active, or confirm their
  TAGA Towers room reservation for free.
  ──────
  #### 2. Price Tampering / No Amount Validation on Order Creation (High)

  • Location: subscription_payment.go:82-140 & tagatower.go:366
  • The Bug:
      • In CreateSubscriptionOrder, fixed-rate subscriptions (e.g. annual-subscription which
      is ₹3,500) do not validate that req.Amount == 350000. A user can modify the API
      request to send amount: 100 (₹1) and get their annual membership activated.
      • Negative or zero amounts are not rejected at the API level, causing unhandled 500
      crashes from the Razorpay SDK.
      • In CreateOrder (Room Booking), the server blindly creates a Razorpay order with
      whatever req.Amount the frontend sends instead of verifying it matches the room's
      calculated advance rate.

  ──────
  #### 3. Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) on Booking Cancellation (High)

  • Location: tagatower.go:190-203
  • The Bug:
  DELETE /api/towers/bookings/:id cancels the booking ID specified in the URL parameter, but
  never checks whether the logged-in user owns that booking.
  • Impact:
  Any authenticated member can cancel other members' room reservations by guessing or
  enumerating booking IDs. In addition, it cancels bookings that are already confirmed/paid
  without any admin restriction.
  ──────
  ### ⚠️ Functional & Business Logic Bugs

  #### 4. Active Membership Revoked When Member Makes a Donation or TBF Contribution (High)

  • Location: Membership.tsx:481-486
  • Code:
    const updatedUser = {
      ...user,
      isPaid: selectedSubscription.id === 'annual-subscription',
      subscription_active: selectedSubscription.id === 'annual-subscription'
    };
    localStorage.setItem("user", JSON.stringify(updatedUser));

  • The Bug:
  If a member who has already paid their annual subscription (isPaid = true) later makes a
  payment for TBF, Legal Fund, or Donation, selectedSubscription.id === 'annual-
  subscription' evaluates to false.
  • Impact:
  The member's active paid status is immediately overwritten to false in their browser
  session, revoking their membership access until they log out and log back in!
  ──────
  #### 5. Webhook Room Booking Amount Displayed in Paise Instead of Rupees (Medium)

  • Location: tagatower.go:541 vs webhook.go:292
  • The Bug:
  In tagatower.go, buildRoomBookingEmailBody expects data.Amount in Rupees (amountInRupees
  := float64(data.Amount)). However, when called from Razorpay Webhook, payment.Amount is in
  Paise (e.g. 10000 paise for ₹100).
  • Impact:
  The booking confirmation email generated via webhook will display ₹ 10,000.00 instead of ₹
  100.00.
  ──────
  #### 6. Duplicate Email Notifications When Webhooks are Active (Medium)

  • Location: subscription_payment.go:370 & webhook.go:200
  • The Bug:
  When a member pays on the website:
      1. /api/subscriptions/verify-payment verifies the signature and sends the payment
      confirmation email.
      2. A few seconds later, Razorpay sends a payment.captured webhook.
      3. Because verify-payment saves to sentPayments in email_helper.go, but webhook.go
      checks processedPayments in payments/processed_payments.json, the webhook does not
      know an email was already sent and dispatches a second duplicate email to the admin.

  ──────
  #### 7. Webhook Does Not Update Database on Browser Disconnect (Medium)

  • Location: webhook.go:230-328
  • The Bug:
  If a payer completes payment on Razorpay but closes their browser before the frontend
  calls /api/subscriptions/verify-payment (e.g. power cut or closed tab), the webhook
  arrives. But webhook.go only sends an email — it never writes to member_subscriptions.json
  or activates the member in members.json, and never confirms the booking in bookings.json.
  • Impact:
  The member is charged, but their subscription or room booking remains unconfirmed in the
  database.
  ──────
  #### 8. Concurrency Race Condition in member_subscriptions.json (Medium)

  • Location: subscription_payment.go:602-615
  • The Bug:
  Unlike tagatower.go which uses bookingsLock.Lock(), saveMemberSubscription and
  updatePaymentTransaction directly perform os.ReadFile followed by os.WriteFile without a
  mutex.
  • Impact:
  If two members submit payments at the exact same millisecond, one write operation can
  overwrite the other, permanently erasing a member's payment record from the JSON file.
  ──────
  #### 9. Context Key Mismatch for Booker Email in tagatower.go (Low)

  • Location: tagatower.go:391 vs member_auth.go:51
  • The Bug:
  In MemberAuthMiddleware, the context stores the email as c.Set("member_email", email). In
  CreateOrder (room booking), it looks for c.Get("email"). This lookup always returns nil.
  (Frontend currently compensates by sending booker details in notes, but the fallback
  lookup fails).
  ──────
  #### 10. Case-Sensitive Email Check on One-Time Fees (Low)

  • Location: subscription_payment.go:63
  • The Bug:
  sub.MemberEmail == email is strictly case-sensitive. If a user enters Member@gmail.com on
  their first payment and member@gmail.com on their second, hasMemberPaidOneTime will return
  false, allowing duplicate one-time fee payments.
  ──────
